PrépaGo Japanese

A kanji learning app with animated stroke order, writing practice, and grade-based progression for international learners of Japanese.

Overview

PrépaGo Japanese is a kanji learning app designed for international learners. It fills the gap in existing apps by providing animated stroke order, writing practice, and a comprehensive grade-based progression system from Grade 1 through 9.

Flutter Dart Kanji JLPT Mobile App

Goal & Story

The project was born from a real observation — a friend could recognize kanji by shape but couldn't write them. This insight drove the creation of an app focused on animated stroke order and hands-on writing practice.

Research & Features

Conducted competitor analysis of existing kanji apps and identified key differentiation points. Translated user insights into core features including animated stroke order, grade-based progression, and contextual learning.
