Brand Background & Strategy

Analyzed Roots' heritage as a nature-inspired Canadian brand and developed a strategy insight connecting holiday gifting with wildlife conservation.

Problem & Target Audience

Identified the current state challenges for Roots and defined the target audience — ethical explorers and conscious Canadian gift-givers.

Persona & Creative Brief

Created user personas and the Sacred Six Creative Brief to guide campaign messaging and creative direction.

Goals & Solution

Defined SMART goals for the Woodland Caribou Collection campaign and detailed the solution including limited-edition products, WWF partnership, and OOH advertising.

Timeline & Roadmap

Planned the campaign rollout from November launch through January follow-up, with a 5-phase roadmap from preparation to annual expansion.
