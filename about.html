About Me

Get to know the person behind the projects.

Hello, I'm Kiriha Aiga

I'm a UI/UX designer, app developer, and motion creator studying Interactive Media Design at Seneca Polytechnic in Toronto. I'm passionate about crafting digital experiences that feel intuitive, beautiful, and meaningful.

With skills spanning UI/UX design (Figma, Illustrator, InDesign), app development (Flutter/Dart, HTML/CSS/JS, Unity 6), and motion design (After Effects, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro), I bring a multidisciplinary lens to every project.

I speak Japanese natively, and English and French at an intermediate level — which drives my interest in multilingual, accessible design. When I'm not designing or coding, I'm building language-learning apps or exploring creative coding with D3.js.
